The Craziest Hike I've ever done

The Hike to Seefelder Joch in Tirol, Austria

Seefelder Joch is 2,060m about 6,760 feet above see level located just Northwest of Innsbruck.

I did this hike with my buddy Tyler while backpacking and exploring Europe over the course of two weeks. The Hike took about six hours to complete and we slept in the snow on the summit.

The Hike

  • Take the train from Innsbruck to Seefeld at 1:00 pm and walk to the start of the hike
  • Hike for 6 hours up the side of the mountain to the Summit. Descend down the back of the summit about 50 feet and set up camp
  • Wake up at 5:00 am to the greatest sunrise and view of your life
